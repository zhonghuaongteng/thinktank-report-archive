Translation

The following document is the current (as of July 29, 2020) draft of an export control bill currently

being considered by China’s parliament, the National People’s Congress. The bill limits exports of

dual-use items, military equipment, nuclear materials, and other goods of counterproliferation

concern, and sets penalties for Chinese exporters who violate the provisions of the bill.
