Translation

The following translation, excerpted from a Chinese government think tank report on China's use of

digital health technology to fight COVID-19, describes several examples of how PRC AI companies

are using AI technology to improve COVID-19 prevention and treatment. Most of the examples, such

as meal delivery robots for hospital staff and technologies that allow doctors to diagnose patients at

a distance, are benign. But a number of these AI applications raise troubling questions about privacy

and human rights, such as tracking of mobile phones used by suspected infected individuals and

video surveillance programs that can identify individuals by posture.
