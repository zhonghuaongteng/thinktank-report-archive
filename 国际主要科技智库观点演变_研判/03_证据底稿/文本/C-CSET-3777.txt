Translation

The following is the text of a speech that Chinese President Xi Jinping delivered to a

symposium of scientists on September 11, 2020, which he had convened to solicit input for

China's upcoming 14th Five-Year Plan. Xi stresses the need for China to improve its

economic and scientific self-sufficiency. To that end, he urges increased investment in basic

research, and advocates both improved academic training for scientists at PRC universities

and continued efforts to recruit foreign scientists to work in China.
