This short Chinese official notice from 2011 provides details on China's "Thousand Talents

Program," specifically the program's procedures for recruiting and retaining non-Chinese foreign

experts who can contribute to China's S&T base.

Chapter 4
Support Measures

Article 11
Experts
under

Thousand
Foreign
Experts

the
Thousand
Talents
Program

Article 12
The

SAFEA

These

Long-term

Foreign

”).

Article 15
SAFEA

and

Thousand
Foreign
Experts

If

Once

The

matching

contract,
part

one-time

and

Article 16
These
