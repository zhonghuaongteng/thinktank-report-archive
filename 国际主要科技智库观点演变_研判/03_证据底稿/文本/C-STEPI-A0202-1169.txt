Summary I SummaryNavigating the Roles of the National R&D Project Teams: Assessing Operational Status and Future ApplicationsㆍProject Leader: Bohun HyunㆍParticipants: Seungyong Shin · Gicheol Jeong · Nari Yoo · Doyun Kim · Junmin Lee · Sungmin Ko · Jaewon Kim · Hyunkeun Bae · Yonghee Cho1. Background and Necessity of Research As the number of various types of National R&D project teams increases in multi-ministerial and large-scale R&D projects, several issues have emerged, including overlapping support, a lack of connectivity, and weakened accountability among project teams. However, there is insufficient research on the status of project teams in national R&D projects and diagnosis of implementation progress. Recently, specific evaluations of the national R&D projects have underscored the need for policy research to assess the appropriateness of the project team's promotion. By investigating recent project team implementation methods and conducting a comprehensive review of associated issues and performance data, we aim to propose a legalization plan related to the project teams. Additionally, we will suggest key considerations during the establishment, operation, and termination stages of project teams.

II 영문 보고서명
2. Operational state of National R&D Project Teams National R&D Project Teams system was introduced in 1999 and has recently been expanded to encompass multi-ministerial and large-scale R&D projects. However, a unified definition and legal basis for the project team remain insufficient. As of 2023, an analysis was conducted on the detailed status of 76 project teams from 11 ministries that responded to the survey. Twenty multi-ministerial project teams were identified, representing a 2.9-fold increase from 2014. In terms of individual ministries, the Ministry of Science and ICT has 16, and the Ministry of Health and Welfare has 13. The ratio of independent corporations and management functions of multi-ministerial project teams is on the rise in terms of organizational form and function. Among the 76 project teams, 13(17.1%) do not have operating regulations, and 12 of them are in the form of internal organizations. The project budget and manpower of the project teams are expanding compared to the past. The 33(43.4%) project teams had undecided plans to close, raising concerns about the potential inadequacy of the post-performance management plan. This could lead to poor spread and utilization of research results.3. Problems Related to National R&D Project Teams The National Assembly and the Board of Audit and Inspection expressed concerns regarding problems associated with the project team system. Major external issues related to the project team are categorized by project promotion stages, including planning, execution and management, performance, and follow-up management. In the planning stage, issues include project team consolidation and minimization, over budgeting, non-conformity with project team objectives, lack of founding basis and unified regulations, and absence of set performance goals.

Summary III
 In the execution and management stage, challenges include a lack of execution, delays in execution, inadequate execution, insufficient experience and capacity of the project team, a lack of project management, and a shortage of manpower. In the performance stage, concerns are raised regarding poor performance and a lack of objectivity in achievements. In the post-management stage, emphasis is placed on the need for follow-up efforts and countermeasures. Challenges faced by project teams include budget and manpower constraints, difficulties in achieving management efficiency, lack of independence and autonomy, absence of regulations, challenges in measuring performance, and difficulties in cooperating and defining roles with related organizations4. Performance Analysis To compare performance based on the project team implementation method, we collected and mapped the current status of projects operating the project team and performance data for each project. In 2021, a total of 893 projects were conducted, with 94 projects (10.53% of the total) promoted by the project team. And performance data utilizes NTIS's 2021 papers, patents, and royalties data. The analysis of performance, using the linear regression analysis method, indicates that project teams generally exhibit a higher tendency in papers performance compared to those not promoted by the project team. However, no significant effects were observed in patents and royalties. Considering the nature of the project, the R&D performance of the project team varies based on factors such as the type of multi-ministerial department, project size, and R&D stage.

IV 영문 보고서명 These results highlight that areas where R&D performance tends to be low may require a more careful and stringent approach when considering project team promotion. However, due to the limitations of the analysis data, there is a constraint on the strict estimation of the causal effect of the project team's promotion method; therefore, attention should be given to the expanded interpretation and utilization of the analysis results.5. Policy Recommendations The insights derived from the analysis of the current status of the project team's implementation method, along with the diagnosis of problems and performance, are systematically classified into planning, execution and management, performance, and follow-up management. The policy Recommendations are as follows:① Review of the legal system related to the project team Unified definitions and common operational regulations for the project team require consensus, and a review of legislative measures, such as amendments to the "National Research and Development Innovation Act," is necessary for formalization.② Suggestions for consideration when establishing, operating, and terminating the project team Establishing review criteria for the formation of a project team is necessary, and applying them to relevant guidelines, such as preliminary feasibility studies and budgeting, is essential. These criteria should serve as the foundation for feasibility judgment. A periodic inspection system for project team operation is required. This can be achieved through current status management via surveys conducted by specialized institutions and evaluation methods embedded in the national R&D assessment system.
