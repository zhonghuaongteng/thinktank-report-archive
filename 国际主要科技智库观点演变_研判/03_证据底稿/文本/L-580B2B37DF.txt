 Concept of deep-tech and the need to support deep-tech startups
 Deep-tech startups refer to companies that build barriers to technological imitation
based on fundamen tal scientific and engineering technologies and translate
laboratory-level knowledge into market-applicable solutions
 Deep tech is characterized by the interdependence among technology, markets, and
regulation, requiring an integrated pipeline that links research, prototyping, pilot
projects, standards and certification, and early demand creation, particularly within
the Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 3–7 range
- After technological validity is demonstrated, investment demand increases sharply,
and large-scale capital financing in later funding rounds becomes a decisive factor for
growth
- This underscores the need for a combination of full-cycle support and growth capital,
as well as the design of qualification linkages and bridge mechanisms between stage

 Policy trends for deep-tech startups in U.S. and Europe
 United States has maintained technological leadership through a long-term, strategic
R&D approach linked to defense and national security, with recent emphasis on supply
chain stabilization
 European countries are systematically fostering deep -tech ecosystems through
government-led large-scale fund formation and long-term strategic planning based on
their respective national strengths

 Policy trends for deep-tech startups by ministry in Korea
 (Ministry of Science and ICT) Promotion of startups based on fundamental
technologies and the development of regionally anchored R&D valleys
- Through programs such as the “Deep Science Startup Promotion Program” and the
“Deep Tech Scale-up Valley Development Program,” the ministry supports early-stage
startups in high-difficulty advanced technology fields and fosters regionally specialized
technology commercialization clusters
 (Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy) Strengthening competitiveness in strategic
industries and reinforcing industrial value chains
- Through initiatives such as the “National Advanced Industry Technology Innovation
Loan Program” and the “Mega -city Cooperation Advanced Industry Dev elopment
Support Program,” the ministry provides policy financing to promote technology
commercialization and encourages joint growth of regionally specialized industries
 (Ministry of SMEs and Startups) Selection of promising firms through private-sector
participation and a public-private cooperation strategy to foster super-gap enterprises
- Through “Deep Tech TIPS” and the “Super Gap 1000+ Project,” the ministry seeks to
enhance private -sector investment and incubation capabilities based on market

 Policy recommendations for supporting Korean deep-tech startups

 Establishing an integrated national control tower for deep-tech startup support and
formulation of a systematic development strategy
- Integration of fragmented, ministry -specific startup support policies and
establishment of a pan -government policy control tower to enhance national
innovation competitiveness
- Establishment of core objectives, performance indicators, and phased
implementation strategies for nurturing and supporting deep -tech startups at the
national level
 Linking national strategic technology innovation with startup support policies
- Enhancing organic linkages between innovation support policies for national core
strategic technologies and startup support policies, and dev eloping differentiated
innovation frameworks by strategic technology domain
 Promoting demand- and problem-driven deep-tech startup development based on
technology convergence
- Promoting technology convergence from a market perspective through a demand -
and problem-based approach and design of market -driven, project-based deep-tech
support programs

Task 1. Strengthening the strategic orientation of deep-tech centered
startup support

 Expansion of large-scale public investment for deep-tech startup growth
- Expansion of government public investment to support deep-tech startup growth and
improvement of investment efficiency through reforms in public fund management
systems
 Regulatory reform to promote demonstration and commercialization of deep- tech
innovation outcomes
- Introduction of tailored regulatory reform systems to reduce regulatory uncertainty
in deep-tech sectors and identify key sector-specific regulatory bottlenecks
- Promotion of effective demonstration and commercialization based on technological
characteristics through integrated operation of fragmented regulatory sandbox
programs and enhancement of follow-up support
 Strengthening the government’s role as an innovation demander through expanded
public procurement
- Strengthening efforts to create early viable markets by expanding public procurement
opportunities for technologies and services developed by innovative deep -tech
startups with high market potential
- Advancement of a Korean-style SBIR/STTR program to strengthen linkages between
SME/startup R&D outcomes and government innovation procurement demand

Task 2. Strengthening the government’s role as a facilitator of deep-tech
innovation commercialization
