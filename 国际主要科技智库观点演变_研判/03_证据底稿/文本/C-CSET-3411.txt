scientific literacy in China, particularly among youth.

Part 1: Overview of the China Association for Science and Technology (CAST)

1.
Primary Responsibilities

Approved
by
the
Communist
Party
of
China
(CPC)

(CAST)

According

​

of

China

Association for Science and Technology,
​
 CAST fulfills the following primary responsibilities:

1.
To

S&T

the
party
line,

and

S&T workers.

2.
To

academic

academic

optimize
the
academic

promote
the
development

3.
To
organize
S&T
workers
to
carry
out
S&T
innovation,

in
scientific

proofs

consulting

innovation

4.
To

popularize
scientific

disseminate

scientific
ideas
and
methods,
defend
the
dignity
of
science,

the

scientific literacy of the nation.

5.
To

the
self-discipline

the
scientific

to

the

integrity

to
advance
scientific

to

a
culture
of
science,
and
to

socialist

core values concept (
社会主

值观
).

6.
To
organize
S&T
workers
to

in
consultation
and

S&T

S&T

7.
To

S&T

the

engineering
and

technical standard development, and national S&T awards and recommendations.

8.
To

outstanding
