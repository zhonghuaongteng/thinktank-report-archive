Translation

The following document is the text of a legally binding agreement between Chinese students who

plan to study abroad and the PRC Ministry of Education's China Scholarship Council (CSC). The

agreement requires students on CSC scholarships to report regularly on the progress of their studies

to the nearest Chinese embassy or consulate and stipulates that scholarship funds will be disbursed

via these same embassies and consulates. It also mandates that students who study abroad must

remain in China to "serve the motherland" for at least two years after they complete their studies

overseas. CSC further demands that each student identify two guarantors who will be held

financially liable if the student breaches the study abroad agreement. CSET has observed several

versions of this or a similar agreement circulating online on the Chinese internet; we selected this

version for translation because it is the most detailed of the bunch.
