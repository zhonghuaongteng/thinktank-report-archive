The K-Innovation Partnership Program was launched in 2022 to strengthen the capacity of
Peruvian civil servants in implementing STI (Science, Technology & Innovation) policies by
sharing methodologies on planning methodology and technology fo recasting. In this
context, the the National Council of Science, Technology and Te chnological Innovation
(hereinafter CONCYTEC) and the Science & Technology Policy Institute (hereinafter STEPI)
have been teaming up, working very closely and intensively so far.
In 2022, STEPI and CONCYTEC staged a series of activities such as a preliminary meeting,
MOU signing ceremony, online kickoff meeting, and virtual ‘Capacity-Building Workshop’,
followed by an onsite ‘Dissemination Workshop’. The most important task for both sides
was to identify the scope, process, contents and timetable of this project. Thanks to diligent
work and versatile dialogue between both sides, this project was able to stay on the right
track, and every activity was completed successfully. The most meaningful event was the
‘Dissemination Workshop’ held on October in Lima. It was the first onsite (offline) workshop
to be held since 2019 in the aftermath of COVID19.
In that workshop, STEPI and CONCYTEC invited a number of Peruvian exp er ts who ha v e
specialties in each of the six priortized sectors. Then, STEPI and CONCYTEC had three-day
practice sessions focusing on technology foresight. The Peruivian experts handpicked the
main drivers that can influence the six prioritized sectors, assessed the impacts of those
drivers, and drafted scenarios that can be envisioned during the sessions. At the same time,
STEPI, CONCYTEC and the Korean Embassy in Peru held joint forums to explain the project
details and share Korea-specific experiences with the Peruvian public.
This year, STEPI and CONCYTEC have endeavored to build up the N ational Technology
Roadmap for priortized sectors on the heels of activities implemented in 2022. First off,

1. Introduction

The K-Innovation Partnership Program is one of Korea’s most prominent ODA programs for
the development of science, technology, and innovation (STI) policies. The program aims to
provide STI policy consultations that cater to the requirements of Korea’s partner countries,
particularly with respect to uti l i z i n g i t s h i g h l y r e c o g n i z e d S TI development models and
extensive experience and offering capacity-building training pr ograms geared toward
effectively designing and implementing the STI policy of its pa rtners for sustainable
development. These policy consultations take place after conducting a selection process for
a number of projects presented b y the applicant countries, with Peru being one of the
selected countries that have presented a project1.
Peru is a wealthy country with ab undant natural resources, and its mining industry is
essential to nationwide economic development. Based on these resources, Peru boasts
strong growth potential and seeks to enhance its national competitiveness by linking science
and technology policies to national economy-linked sectors with strong potential.
With the aim of promoting the STI system and its capacity, the Peruvian government last
y e a r r e f o r m e d t h e L a w o n t h e N a tional System of Science, Techno logy and Innovation
(2021), whose main purpose is to establish a national STI system with the participation of
universities, public research institutes, and companies, including all STI-related entities and
human resources. The newly reformed law also affirms CONCYTEC as the governing body,
whose role is to contribute to the planning of the country's development by formulating STI

2. Objectives

T h i s p r o j e c t f o c u s e s o n C O N C Y T E Css capability to improve the capacity of Peruvian
policymakers and experts to plan R&D programs by sharing STI planning and technological
forecasting methodologies and supporting the creation of technology roadmaps. In addition,
it will be linked to the establishment of a technology foresight-specialized organization, so
as to create capacity building in establishing related policies.
The project objectives consist of the following activities:
1) Activity 1(2022): Development of CONCYTEC’s capacity and institutional building
plan to improve the foundations for S&T planning and technological forecasting for
the prioritized sectors.
2) Activity 2(2023): Creation of the technology roadmap and formulation of the mid-
t o l o n g - t e r m t e c h n o l o g y s t r a t e g y based on technological forecas t i n g f o r t h e
prioritized sectors.
3) Activity 3(2024): Development of the R&D program based on a technology roadmap
for the prioritized sectors and establishment of a permanent ce nter for
technological forecasting.
The theme of the second year in 2023 is to “Build the technology roadmap and mid-/long-
term technology strategy through technological forecasting for the prioritized sectors”.
Based on the current status survey of the six prioritized sectors promoted in the first year
and the technological forecasting capabilities of CONCYTEC researchers and related experts
pertaining to the technology foresight methodology, the goal of the second year is to derive
draft technology roadmaps for the six prioritized sectors and to develop mid- to long-term
technology strategy.

3. Project Framework

The project framework is configured for the purpose of strengthening Peruss capability for
S&T planning and technological forecasting in the prioritized sectors. It will be carried out
over a period of three years with the goal of surveying the current status of Peruvian STI and
sharing the technological forecasting methodology, establishing a technology roadmap and
guidelines for the mid-/long-term technology strategy, planning R&D programs based on
the technology roadmap and estab lishing a specialized instituti on for technological
forecasting.

1.2.2 Theoretical Background of Technology Forecasting Research
The following two applications of technology assessments are denoted as follows.
(1) Technology Foresight
• The UK and Australia carry out technology assessments to select national policy
priorities, with a focus on fundamental science. Policy priorities occasionally include
key technologies or industries.
(2) Technology Forecasting
• South Korea and Japan use technology assessments to promote future-facing science
and technology policies by discovering specific technology tasks. Their policies include
both application and i nfrastructure technologies, and have the characteristics of
mobilizing a large number of experts.

This study mainly applies Korea's industrial technology-related method of technology
forecasting, which is suitable f or the technology prediction ne eds of Peru as a
developing country.
• Technology forecasting activities include gathering opinions fr o m e x p e r t s a n d t h e
general public. A top-down opinion gathering involves a committee consisting of a few
experts, while in a bottom-up opinion approach, a survey is con ducted on a broad
scope of experts.
Future technology planning and re search, including technology forecasting, emphasize
the policy implications in a broad sense and the technology iss ues in a consultation
scope (Kim et al., 2006). In this study, technology forecasting considers the priorities of
n a t i o n a l p o l i c i e s t o d e t e r m i n e t h e p r i o r i t i e s o f t h e m o s t c u t t ing-edge prospetive
technologies (Kim & Lee, 2009). The main factors are analyzed a ccording to the
technology forecasting methodology, and their ripple effects and impacts on technology
policy are evaluated.
• Technology policy research adopts and applies the technique of technology forecasting
in various topics, and here are some examples: prospective technology policy research
in terms of technology commercialization (Kim et al., 2013), sc enario-based future
forecasting through future user analysis (Kim et al., 2011), scenario-based qualitative

2.1 Overview of Technology Roadmap

Technology roadmapping is generally defined as a technology planning method that sets
the technology development goals required to meet future market and customer needs
and that presents the technology and R&D paths required to achi eve them along the
time axis.
• Technology roadmaps can be defined very diversely, depending on the organization,
its purpose, target audience, and its size, by which the proces s (roadmapping) is as
important as the result (roadmap).
• It is expected that technology roadmaps will be perceived as a tool for acquiring
technology. However, another important implication of creating a technology roadmap
is the generation of knowledge inherent to developing a methodology, analysing the
results, and discussing them with experts.

2.2 Technology Road mapping Process
T h e t e c h n o l o g y r o a d m a p d e v e l o p m e n t p r o c e s s c a n b e c a t e g o r i s e d into three stages:
preparation, creation of the technology roadmap, and follow-up
• In the preparation stage, the need for roadmap development, awareness, and a
cooperative atmosphere is created; the roadmap development budget is secured;
and the development organisation is selected. Further, the target market in the
future is researched; roadmap development promotion (draft) is established; a
development committee is formed; and the scope and limitations of the roadmap
are defined.
• The subsequent steps after creating the technology roadmap include verification of the
developed technology roadmap, establishment of an action plan, and periodic revision
and supplementation of the technology roadmap.

The steps in creating a technology roadmap can be broken down i nto the four specific
phases of market definition, pro duct definition, technology def inition, and roadmap
formulation.
• There is no "black box" approach to technology roadmapping, ins tead it entails a
learning process and experience that is built up through some multiple attempts and a
flexible approach to fit the specific situation being considered.
• The T-plan, which is developed by the IfM at the University of Cambridge, is a
standardized approach to support the product planning that consists of four facilitated
workshops. (Phaal, 2000)
• This is the stage where the results of strategic products and t echnology solutions
derived and analyzed in the previous three steps are transferred to a chart layout.
• After conducting a comprehensive review and supplementation of the analyzed data,
a macro roadmap for each core product, or if necessary, a micro-technology roadmap
for each core technology is created.

Korea's technology forecasting activities have been characterized by the following three
features: ① public sector leadership, ② independent implementation by state
ministries & agencies, and ③ diversification of technology forecasting.

activities is that the government and public technology innovation organizations
utilize them primarily as policy tools. In particular, technolo gy innovation
organizations that directly conduct R&D projects on behalf of the government are
conducting technology forecasting activities to achieve specific policy goals.

a c t i v i t i e s a r e a c t i v e o n a n a t i o n a l l e v e l , t h e y a r e c h a r a c t e r i zed by independent

Medium Business Administration, and their activities are very w eakly
interconnected. This is partly attributed to the nature of technology forecasting as
well as the inherent problem of deficient cooperative relationships with the Korean
government.

3.1 Summary
This study has used the methodology that has been developed exclusively for Peru based
on the results collected, discu ssed and agreed upon during the first year of the study,
and carried out technology forecasting based on quantitative da ta and qualitative
information on Peru's science a nd technology activities, politi cal and economic
environment.

been used to conduct technology forescasting activities and let them participate in the
survey so as to analyze the survey result and hold public heari ngs for policy
recommendations.

Utilizing the results of the questionnaire development, a major achievement of the first
year, we conducted an online survey using a survey/questionnaire written in English.

initial plan of 30 experts per area and 180 of them in total, contributing to the statistical
significance of the results. In particular, the response rate was almost 100% due to a
high level of participation and the active efforts of our Peruvian partner organization,
CONCYTEC.

training sessions and two in-person sessions. As for online tra ining, the seminars were
held on August 24 and September 20, respectively, and covered s pecific contents such
as technology forecasting techniques and creation of technology roadmaps.
• In particular, during the period of March 27-31, when Peruvian local experts visited
South Korea, we provided intensive training on technology forecasting and technology
roadmap development, serving as a decisive opportunity to significantly improve the
capabilities of the experts.

3.2 Policy Suggestions & Implications
According to the research proce ss and results of the two-year s tudy in 2022 and 2023,
Peru's S&T environment has several characteristics that disting uish it from the general
situation in developing countries and should be taken into account when proposing and
implementing specific policies in the future.
Therefore, it is advisable that the future-facing policies derived from the results of Peru's
technology forecasting focus on three key policy directions: ①active utilization of
existing industries, ② high government and expert interest in S&T innovation, and ③
securing industrial technology competitiveness in the Latin American region.
• Strengthening the comparative advantages of the existing industries is the most urgent
issue, and it is necessary to exert relentless efforts to secure comparative advantages
in industries with existing comparative advantages, such as agriculture and mining,
across the board.
• To achieve this, in the short term, it is necessary to introduce operation technology and
train personnel in all fields and operating organizations, while in the long term, it is
necessary to relax various regulations, stabilize the exchange rate, and achieve political
stability.

The status of R&D sector and activities in Peru is marked by under-investment, which is
common for developing countries, for a prolonged time. In respo nse, political
determination and policies are needed to reverse this trend.
• Since Peru has a weak industrial base and a very low technology base across the
country, increasing R&D investment is an agenda that applies to all priority areas.
• To achieve policy effectiveness, it is judged to be advisable to simultaneously promote
technology infrastructure projects and invest in technology development to acquire

• However, investments to strengthen the competitiveness of existing industries should

while avoiding expensive mistakes, and even plan for technologies becoming obsolete.

A technology roadmap illustrates the technology presently avail able and emphasizes
scheduled improvements. A technology roadmap should also consider which technologies
will become obsolete or are sche duled for their end of life. Fo r example, a technology
roadmap will show when the previous system will sunset and when the new one will be
implemented.
As organizations lay out their strategy and implementation to help execute on plans for their
vision, a technology roadmap will help them determine which technologies to invest in and
how to implement them. Technolog y roadmaps should facilitate te chnological
infrastructure improvements to meet both long-term and short-term targets of the business
or organization in mind.
The overall function of a technology roadmap is to align your target stakeholders, namely
information technology (IT) and software engineering areas, operations executives, and
even sales teams, by creating a course of action to onboard new technology solutions or

2. Forest Industry3

In this chapter, the status of Peruvian timber industry is analyzed. Peru ranks fourth in the
world in terms of tropical forest coverage, with more than 67 million hectares in tropical
forest area (MINAM, 2022).

2.1 Current Status

2.1.1 National production of timber products
The national production of primary processed timber products is based on the harvesting
and processing of hardwood from tropical rainforest species. Tropical rainforests have a
great diversity of species, which have different characteristics to be considered during the
processing process. In addition, timber extraction takes place during harvests that depend
on the rainy season (based on a seasonal activity), so the supply of raw material for the
timber industry is uncertain and inconsistent. Even more so, we must take into account the
following: During the period fr om 2001 – 2021, 2.7 million hect ares of forests were
deforested (MINAM, 2022), the years 2020 and 2021 already surpa ssed the record
deforestation rates for the last 20 years, and no less than 50% of the remaining tropical
rainforests are undergoing a degradation process (CNF, 2022), that is, losing the capacity to
regenerate their plant and animal species, with the subsequent loss of their ecosystem
services and commercial timber forest species. The experts point out that the supply of raw
material for the Peruvian timber industry in the future will depend on the expansion of high
productivity commercial forestry plantations (CNF, 2022). Such a market is different from
the hardwood market, and the learning curve could worsen the pl ight of the Peruvian
timber industry.
According to Peruvian forestry legislation, timber products are classified as either first
transformation or second transformation products. (SERFOR-MIDAG RI, 2015). SERFOR

All of this endows Peruvian citizens not only a cultural legacy but also the spirit of research

3.2.1 Competitiveness
The textile industry occupies a decreasing share of Peru's non-traditional exports, as it is
affected by strong competition from Asian countries. Therefore, it is necessary to diversify
exports by focusing on products with greater added value.
By guiding the sector's innovation efforts, the TRM would allow the identification of key
technologies for automating processes, reducing times and costs , and increasing
productivity throughout the entire value chain, with the focus on key areas such as new
materials, design and dyeing processes, traceability, etc.
The industry involves small, rural producers of camelid fibers – increased competitiveness
would enable their inclusion in the global economy since fine hair camelid fibers such as
alpaca and vicuña are highly valued worldwide. Training aimed at increasing productivity is
also needed, such as camelid management techniques, fiber classification, dyeing, garment
design, etc.
The TRM can identify and determine which environmentally-friendly technologies should
be implemented at each stage of the production process.

The associativity leading to collaboration among producers with in cooperatives and
consortia is the key to integration into production chains in order to access external markets
and reduce costs.
The TRM can also determine how to apply technologies for greater integration between
links in the textile supply chain.

Competitiveness is limited by low productivity, high labor and logistics costs, and
weaknesses in the production chain. Therefore, investment is needed in infrastructure and
logistics, regional clusters, company coordination, and greater added value.
Technologies such as RFID and information systems can improve logistics traceability and
efficiency.

The industry has disadvantages in terms of logistics costs and raw materials. Vertical
integration of the productive chain and improvement of the logistics and port infrastructure
are required.

4.1.7 Mid- & long-term strategies & case studies for technology foresight
 R&D mining processes
To understand intricately the mining processes of research and development, it is
imperative to know why there are no technological improvements in mining. First, since the
arrival of Spanish conquerors in the 16th century, Peru was a source of precious metals like
silver and gold. The population was exploited without improving their quality of life. Even
until today, their communities st ill yearn for social improveme nts. In many cases,
communities are reluctant to embrace the construction of new refineries due to pollution
to their environment. For this reason, it is noteworthy for the first steps to promote win-win
trades with local communities. For example, solar energy, clean extraction of metal and
minerals, environmental damage control are well-embraced prospe cts for these
communities.

4.1.8 Major Challenges & Suggestions
In the particular case of mining, the major challenge is not technological but diplomatic.

1. Wrap-up of 2nd Year Activities
During the course of economic gr owth, the technology has played a c r i t i c a l r o l e b y
generating new economic values from producing new products and services. That’s why
almost all countries have strived to build up technological capabilities by investing on R&D

necessary financial resources for R&D activities and set up appropriate R&D programs even

When they set up the R&D program and make a budget, they have to have very specific
guidelines such as target technologies, quantitative goals and detailed timeline. That’s why
we have utilized the methodology of technology foresight/foreca sting and technology
roadmap. By conducting technology foresight/forecasting, we can d e s i g n a t e t a r g e t
technologies that we have to secure to foster industrial progress. At the same time, the
technology roadmap has been used to formulate timeline of R&D activities and financial
input and to set up the quantitative goals.

methodologies and previous cases of technology foresight with P eruvian partners. In
addition, the STEPI Team held the practice session on October 2022 in Lima as a part of the

become familiarized with the methodologies and accumulate necessary knowledge. They

on the industrial sector, target technology, and business characteristics. Nevertheless,
there exist theoretical principles and basic procedures which are in common. In this
respect, the technology roadmap has been widely utilized by not only business
enterprises but also by public entities including the government.
In 2023, STEPI and CONCYTEC have jointly taken the required step s for constru cting t he

Dissemination Workshop on October respectively. During the courses, Peruvian experts
became familiarized with the methodologies and succeeded in compiling the draft of the
technology roadmap themselves.
In addition, the STEPI Team along with SUNY Korea team conducted the Delphi survey to
figure out technological capabilit i e s u s i n g t h e T R L ( t e c h n o l o g y Readiness Level) criteria,

Welcome to the Delphi survey for Peru's technology roadmap!

We appreciate your participation in shaping the future of technology in Peru. This survey
aims to gather valuable insights and expert opinions to develop a c o m p r e h e n s i v e
roadmap that will guide the country's technological advancements.
Throughout this survey, we will explore various aspects of technology in Peru, including
emerging trends, key challenges, potential opportunities, and p riority areas for
investment. Your input will play a crucial role in identifying the most promising
technologies and formulating effective strategies to drive sustainable growth and socio-
economic development.
We encourage you to provide thoughtful and well-informed responses based on your
knowledge and experience. Your expertise will be invaluable in creating a roadmap that
aligns with Peru's unique needs and aspirations.
Thank you for your time and comm itment to advancing technology i n P e r u . Y o u r
contributions will make a significant impact in shaping the country's future and ensuring
a prosperous digital era for all its citizens.

From Technology Forecasting Project Team,
CONCYTEC of PERU, collaborated with STEPI and SUNY Korea of South Korea

[Notice] This survey may take 20-30 minutes. Please allow enough time to complete this form.

This survey for technology forecasting consists of three parts:
• Part A is to collect the basic information for creating technology roadmaps.
• Part B has 8 questions on technology and policy choices, and its results will be analyzed
to reach the consensus for the policy with the Delphi method.
• Part C has open-ended questions for your suggestion or opinions.
