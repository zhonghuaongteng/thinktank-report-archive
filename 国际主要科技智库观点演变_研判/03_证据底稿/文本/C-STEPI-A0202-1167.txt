Summary I SummaryA Study on Amendment of Income Tax for Employees’ Invention CompensationㆍProject Leader: Hakhyo KimㆍParticipants: Sohn, Soo J. · Yong-gi Kim1.Background and Necessity of ResearchCompensation for employees’ invention was exempt until 2016. However, with the amendment of the income tax law at the end of 2016, it has been subject to income tax since 2017. At the time of the amendment to the income tax law, the government was reorganizing the tax exemption and exemption systems to reduce the ratio of tax-exempt people, which had increased due to various exemptions and exemptions, and to expand the tax base. By the government's tax policy direction, income taxation on compensation for employees’ invention began in 2017.As income taxation begins on employees’ invention compensation, there are concerns about researchers' enthusiasm for research and invention. Additionally, there are worries that this could weaken technological competitiveness.Therefore, this study aims to identify ways to improve the income tax system for employees’ invention compensation, enhancing researchers' enthusiasm for research and improving technological competitiveness.In deriving improvement measures, we will analyze the changes in the tax burden and income tax revenue. Additionally, through a survey, we derive measures to improve the income tax system that researchers in the research field can perceive.

IV A Study on Amendment of Income Tax for Employees’ Invention Compensation 3. Policy Recommendations 1) Income Classification RevisionA revision is necessary to classify employees’ invention compensation as other income regardless of employment status. Employees’ invention compensation is the compensation received in return for transferring the rights to an employees' invention to the employer, so it is difficult to consider it as earned income. Therefore, it is necessary to revise the Income Tax Act to classify compensation for employees’ inventions as other income rather than earned income. 2) Application of Separate TaxationCompensation for employees’ inventions may result in a progressive tax burden because income is concentrated at a particular time. So, it is necessary to apply separate taxation to alleviate the progressive tax burden. The current taxation system is structured so that as the compensation for employees’ inventions increases, the marginal tax rate also increases, resulting in a progressive tax burden. However, in the case of separate taxation, the withholding tax rate of 20% is applied regardless of the employees’ invention compensation amount so that the progressive tax burden can be alleviated. 3) Increase in Tax Exemption AmountIf separate taxation is implemented, the income tax burden for low-income workers may increase compared to the current system. Low-income workers are subject to a marginal tax rate of 6% or 15% under the current tax system. Therefore, if separate taxation is implemented, the tax exemption amount (currently 5 million won) must be increased simultaneously. If the tax exemption amount is increased to 20 million won, only workers whose marginal tax rate is higher than the withholding tax rate of 20% will be subject to taxation. Furthermore, if the tax exemption amount is set at 20 to 40 million won, approximately 90 to 96% of workers who reported employees’ invention compensation are expected to not pay income tax on the employees’ invention compensation.
