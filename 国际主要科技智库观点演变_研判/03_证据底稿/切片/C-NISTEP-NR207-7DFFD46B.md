Report on the Japanese National Innovation Survey 2024 (J-NIS 2024)
National Institute of Science and Technology Policy (NISTEP), MEXT
Abstract
 The Japanese National Innovation Survey 2024 (J-NIS 2024), which follows the Oslo Manual
2018 – the international guidelines for innovation statistics, is a voluntary survey conducted by NISTEP
as a governmental institution. Frame population of the survey consists of 441,303 enterprises with ten or
more persons employed. Sample size was 32,489 enterprises by using stratified random sampling. V alid
responses were obtained from 12,577 enterprises (unweighted unit response rate: 39%) .
 Main findings from the survey results are as follows: During the three years from 2021 to 2023,
36% of the enterprises in the frame population (i.e. 156,960 enterprises) realised innovations, up 4%
points compared to the previous survey round (reference period: 2019–2021). Specifically, 12% (i.e.
51,733 enterprises) and 32% (i.e. 139,585 enterprises) realised product innovation and business process
innovation, respectively. 10% of the product-innovative enterprises introduced new-to-world products
into their markets. In addition, 17% of the enterprises in the frame population realised innovations with
environmental benefits. Moreover, 51% (i.e. 239,270 enterprises) implemented innovation activities. 3%
of the innovation-active enterprises cooperated with universities or other higher education institutions
for innovation activities.
