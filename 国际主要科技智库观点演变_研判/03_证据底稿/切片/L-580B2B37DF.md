STEPI Insight | VOL.356

Global Deep-Tech Startup Support Policies and Implications for Korea
Eunji Mok· Younghwan Kim

 Concept of deep-tech and the need to support deep-tech startups
 Deep-tech startups refer to companies that build barriers to technological imitation
based on fundamen tal scientific and engineering technologies and translate
laboratory-level knowledge into market-applicable solutions
 Deep tech is characterized by the interdependence among technology, markets, and
regulation, requiring an integrated pipeline that links research, prototyping, pilot
projects, standards and certification, and early demand creation, particularly within
the Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 3–7 range
- After technological validity is demonstrated, investment demand increases sharply,
and large-scale capital financing in later funding rounds becomes a decisive factor for
growth
- This underscores the need for a combination of full-cycle support and growth capital,
as well as the design of qualification linkages and bridge mechanisms between stage

 Policy trends for deep-tech startups in U.S. and Europe
 United States has maintained technological leadership through a long-term, strategic
R&D approach linked to defense and national security, with recent emphasis on supply
chain stabilization
 European countries are systematically fostering deep -tech ecosystems through
government-led large-scale fund formation and long-term strategic planning based on
their respective national strengths
SUMMARY
