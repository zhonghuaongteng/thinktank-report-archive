Executive Summary

The goals of the K-Innovation ODA Program are to build each partner country ’s
capacity to formulate STI policy for implementing the project , with the s pecific aim of
promoting the project’s purposes . The K -Innovation ODA Program’s profe ssional and
academic experience in designing strategies have enabled the Kingdom of Cambodia
(Cambodia) project to produce both the national strategies and technology roadmaps.
This is the third year of phase 2 of the K -Innovation project with Cambodia. Phase 1 of
the project was carried out from 2018 to 2019 under the title of ‘Policy Consultation
on the Development of a National STI Policy and Management of the STI System for
Cambodia's Policy makers’. During the second year of phase 2 in 2021, it was
recognized that the project had produce d some meaningful outcomes despite the
restrictions caused by the COVID-19 pandemic. Based on the experience s and lessons
learned from the first year, the project’s third year was effectively conducted in similar
ways to the second year, utilizing online meetings and drawing on support from local
institutions in Cambodia. In addition to the way the project is being operated, STEPI
and the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology & Innovation (MISTI) of the Royal
Government of Cambodia have concluded a service contract to develop the first
national technology roadmap of Cambodia.

process and ceremony, and a training workshop were held online or via e-mail.
