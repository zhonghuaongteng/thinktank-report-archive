advancement of information and communication technology is changing industrial structure
and IT is a crucial factor for industrial competitiveness of a company.

expectations for science, technology and innovation (STI) in society are also growing. This is
supposed to be reflecting the fluctuation of the values mentioned above. As policy measures
for realizing innovation, it is getting clear to integrate relevant S&T policies into an innovation
ecosystem and promote them as a whole.
