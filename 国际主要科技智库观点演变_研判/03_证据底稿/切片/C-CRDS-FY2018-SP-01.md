Executive Summary
Society of the 21st century faces challenges which are complicated and expanding on
a global scale, and the inﬂuence that new technologies brings in the society becomes
larger as never before. In these situation, to ﬁnd out human dignity and the future
direction of our society, we need to work on these difﬁcult missions based on diverse
views and knowledge of multiple ﬁelds. Particularly, in the discussion on utilization
of new technologies including artiﬁcial intelligence ʢAIʣ and advanced biotechnology,
in which the signiﬁcant value judgment for human being is required, the views and
the knowledge of both natural sciences and social sciences and humanities ʢSSHʣ are
signiﬁcant.
Under the awareness of challenges mentioned above, necessity of collaboration
between natural sciences and SSH receives greater recognition among those in the
community of research and innovation policy. In the 5th Science and Technology Basic
Plan of Japan ʢ2016-2020ʣ, the necessity of collaboration between both field are
described in several sections including that of Society 5.0

Development Goals ʢSDGsʣ of United Nations, the collaboration between natural
sciences and SSH is regarded as an essential element. In Horizon 2020, the research
and innovation program of Europe Union ʢEUʣ from 2014 to 2020, the participation
from SSH are embedded in some of the calls of the program. As a trend in international
academies, International Council of Science ʢICSUʣ, the academic organization of
natural sciences, and International Social Science Council ʢISSCʣ was merged to
International Science Council ʢISCʣ in July 2018.
In Japan, however, the necessity of collaboration between natural sciences and
SSH has been just mentioned as a basic and general concept of policy-making and
the practical measures to stimulate collaboration between both ﬁelds have not been
considered to the present. Consequently, Center for Research and Development
Strategy ʢCRDSʣ of Japan Science and Technology Agency ʢJSTʣ started to investigate
ʠthe measures for cooperation ʡ to stimulate the collaboration between both fields
