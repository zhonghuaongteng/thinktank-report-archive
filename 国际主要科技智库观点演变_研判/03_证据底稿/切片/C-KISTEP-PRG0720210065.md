SUMMARYⅠ. Title : A study on the Establishment of Guidelines for Field Application of Basic Research Support System by Academic SectorⅡ. Purpose and necessity of Research and Development (R & D)◯In order to successfully establish the support system for each academic field in a researcher-led basic research project, it is necessary to prepare a mid- to long-term support plan and investment strategy for basic research in the future.-In the field application of the support system for each academic field, it is necessary to derive a mid- to long-term support plan through exchange of opinions and discussions on the reflection of demand in the academic field and the direction of development.◯It is essential to establish a guideline for field application of support systems for each academic field that reflects essential considerations when allocating budgets for basic research projects by field.Ⅲ. Content and scope of R & D◯Research on the status of support systems by academic field for researcher-led basic research by country-Analysis of the status of investment in basic research projects led by domestic researchers and the status of pilot implementation of support systems by academic field-Comparison of researcher-led basic research support systems in major overseas countries such as the United States, Japan, United Kingdom, and China and research on the status of support systems by academic field◯Evaluation of the performance of the basic research support system by domestic and foreign academic fields and diagnosis of key issues- In the basic research project led by domestic researchers, the performance evaluation of the pilot introduction of the support system for each academic field and the diagnosis of key issues according to the pilot application-Excavation of excellent success factors that can be benchmarked against domestic support systems for each academic field through case analysis of major foreign countries◯Research on establishing guidelines for field application of support systems by academic fields in basic research projects led by domestic researchers-Derivation of considerations for successful establishment of support systems for each academic field in researcher-led basic research projects-Derivation of a plan to expand and apply the support system for each academic field-Establishment of guidelines for field application of support systems for each academic field reflecting the derived considerations
