The title of the 2022 K -Innovation Partnership Program with Lao PDR is the “National
Master Plan for STI R&D and Application in Lao PDR.” As a three -year project (2020 -
2022), the project aims to support the development of the National STI Master Plan of
Lao PDR. For the last two years, our STEPI -MTC(Ministry of Technology and ICT in Lao
PDR) team has focused on analyzing the national innovation system in Lao PDR and
sharing STI development experiences and current issues of both countries. In 2021, our
team particularly explored public STI research institutes, higher education and university
systems, and the STI roles in the agriculture and biotech sectors of both Korea and Lao
PDR, and published the report, which was presented to the national government of Lao
PDR.
In order to develop a framework and drafts of the National STI Master Plan of Lao PDR
by the end of the final year of the project, STEPI and MTC collaboration have placed the
top two priorities in 2022 in 1) policy consultation in three strategic areas —technology
commercialization, SME innovation, and international STI collaboration —, 2) capacity
building of Laotian government officials in STI policy planning through a series of
workshops and seminars held throughout the year of 2022 in Korea and Lao PDR.
This report is the output of a year -long collaboration between STEPI and MTC on STI
policy consultation and a series of capacity -building workshops. Starting with an
