on” in January 2024, announcing that the three elements of science and technology diplomacy 
proposed in 2010 will be reviewed on various occasions over the next year. A special issue of 
Science Diplomacy was published in October 2024, and a new report, “Science Diplomacy in an 
Era of Disruption,” was issued in February 2025. The report proposed a simplified framework 
with two dimensions: Science impacting diplomacy: How science relates to diplomatic 
objectives, and Diplomacy impacting science: How diplomacy engages and influences scientific 
activities. The report laid out three important points for the practice of science diplomacy that 
aims to simplify and create a more flexible framework that is adaptable to current and future 
challenges: (1) Science diplomacy is an important tool to promote international relations, (2) 
Science diplomacy is increasingly being used by non-state actors, and (3) Science diplomacy is 
required in times of turmoil. (See Column 1 for a summary of the report.)
