ABSTRACT

Economic globalisation has given rise to two types of networks that stretch out across OECD and
emerging economies. At the one side, Global Value Chains (GVCs) can be thought of as the
“material” transfers of goods and services (final as well as intermediate) across borders. At the other
side, Global Innovation Networks (GINs) refer to the transfers of intangibles and immaterial assets
between countries. Concerns are increasingly raised in policy discussions that countries are not able to
capture the value of their innovative activities, hence the clear need to better understand the
interdependencies between these two types of networks.
