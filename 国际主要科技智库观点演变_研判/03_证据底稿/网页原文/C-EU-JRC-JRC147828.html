Quantum Technologies. Comparative Analysis of Media Narratives and the Scientific Landscape
2026Technical reportsEmbracing the AI data-driven era​ Innovation for Democracy and Public Governance Foresight, support to science and innovative policymaking
The purpose of this report is to explore how Quantum Technologies are represented across mainstream and information manipulation sources published between 1 December 2024 and 27 April 2026 in the EU, the US, Russia, and China, using text mining techniques. The analysis aims to provide an overview of the narratives, reporting trends, and sentiment and framings impacting public discourse on quantum technologies.
REITIS-MUNSTERMANN Theresa ; DAMJANOVSKI Aleksandar ; KOTSEVA Bonka ; BERGAMINI Michela; CAIELLI Andrea; KOVACIKOVA Kristina
