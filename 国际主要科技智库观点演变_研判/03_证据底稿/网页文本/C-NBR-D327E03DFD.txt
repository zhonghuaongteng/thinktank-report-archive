NBR Special Report no. 75
Charting a Path for a Stronger U.S.-Japan Economic Partnership

By Saori N. Katada, Junji Nakagawa, and Ulrike Schaede
February 14, 2019

This NBR Special Report from the Center for Innovation, Trade, and Strategy explores how the United States and Japan can strengthen their economic relationship and work together to promote a rules-based, liberal economic order for the Asia-Pacific.
