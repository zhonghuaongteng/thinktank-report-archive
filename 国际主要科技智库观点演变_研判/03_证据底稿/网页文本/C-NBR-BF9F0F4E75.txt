U.S.-Japan Economic and Technology Security Cooperation

Roundtable with Daisuke Kawai, Crystal Pryor , and Kazuto Suzuki
January 20, 2025

In recent years, challenges including supply chain shortages, escalating geopolitical tensions, and rapid technological advances that carry potential dual-use risks have caused leaders in Japan and the United States to re-examine existing paradigms regarding economic security and resiliency in critical sectors. This roundtable brings together U.S. and Japanese experts to discuss the two countries’ approaches to enhance the resiliency of their economies and societies and thereby achieve economic security.

Reinventing Japan’s Economic Security: Balancing Interdependence with Strategic Technology
Daisuke Kawai
U.S. and Japanese Economic Security Policies: Promoting and Protecting Advanced Technologies
Crystal Pryor
The Japanese Semiconductor Renaissance: Will It Be Successful?
Kazuto Suzuki
