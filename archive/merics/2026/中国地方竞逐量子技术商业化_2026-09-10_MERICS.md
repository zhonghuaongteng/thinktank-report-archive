# 中国地方竞逐量子技术商业化

## 中文摘要与研判

### 核心观点

MERICS认为，中国地方政府正在以应用场景、产业基金和首用补贴推动尚未成熟的量子技术从实验室走向市场，这延续了地方竞争式产业政策。广东提出覆盖医疗、海洋与金融科技的100个场景，广州开展心血管筛查和药物分子模拟试点，上海对首批商业化产品给予最高2000万元支持，四川、湖北和北京也投入不同规模的政府资本。文章并不认为这些试验都会成功：量子传感在安徽电网试点中仅小幅优于传统方案，工程、标准和成本仍限制规模化。其判断是，即使部分项目缺乏商业可行性，早期试错仍能帮助中央识别高价值应用并积累中长期竞争优势；反方约束在于，地方补贴无法替代企业在技术成熟度和成本上的实质突破。

### 建议

地方量子政策应把场景验证、工程标准和成本评估置于简单扩基金之前，用可比较的试点数据筛选真正可规模化的应用，并防止各地重复培育同类“冠军”。

### 中国/上海参考

文章直接提到上海量子计算枢纽以最高2000万元支持首批产品落地，可作为研究上海“首用—场景—产业化”工具的外部观察样本；但材料未评估补贴绩效，不能据此判断项目已经商业成功。

picture alliance / Xinhua News Agency | Dai Rui Share this Article Comment Sep 10, 2026 2 min read Provinces race to commercialize quantum technology research China aims to lead in quantum – even as many quantum technologies are not yet mature. Provinces and cities are already competing to build markets for a range of applications. Most of these experiments are unlikely to pan out commercially as the technology is still nascent, and costs remain too high. But for Beijing, even unsuccessful or financially unfeasible applications show where quantum tech can prove most useful – an early bet that may pay off as a competitive advantage in the medium to long term. Guangdong province recently introduced 100 application scenarios in eight areas, including medical diagnosis, marine exploration, and fintech, which could lay the foundation for the mass implementation of quantum technologies in China. Hospitals and the national lab in Guangzhou are already piloting quantum-enabled screening for cardiovascular disease and molecular simulation for drug discovery. The stakes for medicine are high, as Chinese researchers argue that quantum computing could cut drug development timelines from ten to three years, given quantum’s edge in precise molecular simulation. Other localities have been early supporters of commercialization. Shanghai’s new quantum computing hub offers up to CNY 20 million (EUR 2.57 million) to companies bringing the first products to market in an area where China lags behind the US. Sichuan, Hubei and Beijing have each pooled capital in the past few months (ranging from CNY 100 million to CNY 1 billion, mostly from state investors) to accelerate quantum technologies from lab to market. Most applications remain experimental. Recently, in Anhui province, quantum sensors were used to keep the power grid reliable as it monitored tiny changes in temperature inside a switchgear. The results outperformed traditional sensors, but not by much, leaving open whether a broader rollout would be worth the cost. This shows that years of R&D have delivered precision gains, but moving quantum sensors from laboratories into operation brings challenges around engineering and standards. As some applications mature, China aims to unite progress nationwide to scale the industry’s development. MERICS Analyst Altynay Junusova : “This is the standard Chinese industrial policy playbook, where provinces roll out competing policies to pursue tech self-reliance, each hoping to cultivate their own national champions.
