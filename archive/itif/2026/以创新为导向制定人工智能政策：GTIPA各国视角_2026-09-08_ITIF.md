# 以创新为导向制定人工智能政策：GTIPA各国视角

## 中文摘要与研判

### 核心观点

报告把人工智能视为可能像电力和互联网一样广泛扩散的通用技术，但同时承认其能力边界和演进方向仍有很大不确定性。它汇集30个国家28家智库的32个案例，展示各国政府如何通过创新政策支持AI开发和应用，既包括已经实施的措施，也包括尚待兑现的政策愿景。GTIPA的总体立场是，只要治理得当，AI可以推动增长与发展，并主张民主国家在开放、透明、责任和创新之间形成共同框架。报告附带23个签署方支持的“开放AI宣言”，反对政府审查和操纵模型，强调表达自由、贸易开放和跨国合作；这一规范性立场反映联盟成员的共同价值取向，不等同于对各国政策成效的统一评估。

### 建议

建议民主国家围绕开放获取、透明度、责任使用和国际协作建立AI政策框架，同时根据各国制度与发展条件比较具体工具，避免把案例汇编当作单一路径处方。

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the general-purpose technology of this decade, and possibly even this century, with impacts predicted to be as deep and diffuse as electricity and the Internet. As with other general-purpose technologies in their nascent stages, much still remains uncertain regarding AI’s capacity and direction. This report addresses the state of innovation policy on AI around the world, compiling 32 vignettes from 28 think tanks in 30 countries—Albania, Argentina, Australia, Bangladesh, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Brazil, Bulgaria, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador, Germany, Greece, India, Indonesia, Italy, Kosovo, Mexico, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Pakistan, the Philippines, Poland, Peru, Serbia, South Africa, Sweden, the United States, and Vietnam. It details the impactful measures that these governments have taken to drive successful development and deployment of AI in their economies. The countries highlighted in this report are diverse, representing every continent except Antarctica and a range of economic and social contexts. Some of these vignettes portray triumphs already won, some outline visions yet to be realized. All of them support GTIPA’s belief that, if governed wisely, AI can make substantial contributions to global growth and development. This report also includes a Declaration of Open AI, which calls for democratic countries to establish a global framework for free and open AI, protecting freedom of expression, innovation, free trade, and access to AI while promoting transparency and responsible use. The declaration, which is endorsed by 23 signatories, argues that governments should avoid censoring or manipulating AI and should instead pursue international collaboration to ensure AI remains accessible, innovative, and aligned with democratic values.
